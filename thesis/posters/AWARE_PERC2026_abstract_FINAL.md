**Title:** AWARE: a context-aware framework for identifying community cultural wealth in STEM reflective writing

**Authors:** Khalid Mehtab Khan, Anagha Kulkarni, Kim Coble (San Francisco State University); Khanh Q. Tran (Utah State University)

## Abstract (150 words)

At San Francisco State University, a Hispanic-Serving Institution, the Alma project invites students in introductory physics and astronomy labs to journal on "Why am I here?" Alma reflective journaling draws out the Community Cultural Wealth (CCW) that marginalized students bring into STEM, but its analysis at scale has remained a manual process, with researchers hand-labeling each sentence for which of eight kinds of cultural wealth it expresses. Across 2,710 hand-labeled essays, this writing presents several challenges: a sentence's meaning depends on the surrounding essay, themes overlap, and some appear far more often than others. We present AWARE (Adaptive Weighted Annotation for Reflective Essays), which learns how students write, reads each sentence within its whole essay, and targets the rarest themes. AWARE flags sentences for human review—an assistant to coders, not a replacement—helping most with the rarest, hardest-to-find themes. A dashboard supports exploration, annotation with AWARE's suggestions, and training new coders.
